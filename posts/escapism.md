Escapism is an art. It’s a skillful blend of satisfaction and emptiness. 

We escape in the inbetween.   
Not quite busy, but not quite bored.  
Not quite awake, but not quite asleep.  
Not quite crazy, but not quite calm. 

The uncertainty of our conscious state distracts from the uncertainty in our subconscious state.

Don’t let escapism become a constant or default state of being. 

Busy    | Bored   
Awake | Asleep  
Crazy  | Calm  
           ↓  
    Escapism
